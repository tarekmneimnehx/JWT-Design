JWT Design Studio — Architecture & Interior Design

JWT Design Studio is an architecture and interior design studio founded in 2004 by sisters Jinan and Joelle Touma, with studios in Dubai and Beirut. We design residential, commercial and hospitality spaces across the GCC and MENA region — from luxury villas and majlis to offices, retail, hotels and restaurants — and carry projects from concept to completion.

Our disciplines

  • Architecture — full architectural design and visualisation
  • Interior Design — residential, commercial and hospitality interiors
  • Lighting Design
  • Landscape Design

What we design

  • Luxury villa and residential interior design in Dubai, Abu Dhabi and the wider UAE
  • Office and commercial interior design
  • Hospitality interior design — hotels, cafés and lounges
  • Architecture and landscape design

Where we work

United Arab Emirates (Dubai, Abu Dhabi), Saudi Arabia (Riyadh), the wider GCC, Lebanon (Beirut) and Syria (Aleppo).
