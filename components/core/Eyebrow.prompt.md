The JWT eyebrow — a small uppercase tracked mono label that sits above headings and section titles.

```jsx
<Eyebrow dot>Our Expertise</Eyebrow>
<Eyebrow tone="inverse">Selected Projects</Eyebrow>
```

`tone`: `accent` (taupe, default), `muted`, `ink`, `inverse` (for dark sections). `dot` adds the brand taupe dot.
